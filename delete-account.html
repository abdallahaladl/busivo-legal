Delete your Busivo account and data

You can ask us to delete your Busivo account and the personal data linked to it at any time.

How to request deletion

  1. Email abdallah.aladl.dev@gmail.com from the phone number or email address associated with your account.
  2. Use the subject line “Busivo account deletion request”.
  3. Include your account phone number, your role (parent, driver, or school), and the school or bus you are associated with so we can verify the request.
  4. We will confirm the request and complete the deletion within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your account profile: name, phone number, role, and account code.
  • Your authentication data and push-notification token.
  • Messages and leave requests associated with your account, unless the school is legally required to keep them.
  • Crash and analytics records are removed or anonymized according to Firebase retention settings.

What may be kept

  • School-managed student, bus, and trip records may be retained by the school that owns them. The school is responsible for those records; contact the school to request their deletion.
  • Records we must keep to comply with legal, tax, or safety obligations, for the period required by law.
  • Aggregated or anonymized statistics that no longer identify you.

Delete some data without deleting your account

You can also email us to correct or delete specific information (for example, a phone number or a message) without closing your account. Use the same address and explain what you want removed.
