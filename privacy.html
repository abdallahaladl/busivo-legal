Privacy Policy — Busivo

Effective date: October 31, 2026 Last updated: October 31, 2026

Busivo ("we", "our", or "us") provides a school bus tracking and communication service for schools, bus drivers, and parents. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. It applies to the Busivo Android application and the related backend service.

1. Who uses Busivo

Busivo accounts are created by adults:

  • Schools manage buses, drivers, students, and routes.
  • Drivers share their live location while a bus trip is active and update student boarding status.
  • Parents view their children's bus, receive notifications, and request leave.

Busivo is not directed to children. Children do not create accounts, and we do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children. Student information is entered by the school or parent who is responsible for the student.

2. Information we collect

### Account information

  • Name, phone number, role (school, driver, or parent), and account code.
  • For schools: school name and address.
  • For drivers: driver license number and assigned school/bus.
  • Authentication is performed with a one-time code sent to your phone number.

### Student information

  • Student name, grade, school, assigned bus, parent account, pickup/drop-off coordinates, and trip status (on bus, dropped off, absent, on leave).
  • This information is provided by the school or parent, not collected from the student.

### Location information

  • Drivers: precise location (including in the background while a trip is active) is used to show the bus on the map to the school and to parents of students on that bus. Background location is only collected while the driver has started a trip and a persistent notification is displayed.
  • Parents and schools: location is not collected; the map is used to view the bus.

### Device and usage information

  • Firebase Cloud Messaging token, device platform, app version, and app language.
  • Crash reports and anonymous analytics through Firebase Crashlytics and Firebase Analytics (device model, OS version, app version, crash stack traces, and general feature usage).

### Communications

  • Messages exchanged through the in-app messaging feature between schools, drivers, and parents.
  • Leave requests submitted by parents.

3. How we use information

We use information to:

  • Authenticate users and provide the tracking, student management, messaging, and leave-request features.
  • Show the live position of a bus to authorized users.
  • Send notifications about arrivals, trip changes, and school messages.
  • Diagnose crashes, improve reliability, and understand feature usage.
  • Comply with legal obligations and enforce our Terms of Service.

4. Legal bases

Where required by law, we process information based on:

  • Contract: to provide the service you or your school requested.
  • Legitimate interests: to keep the service secure, reliable, and improved.
  • Consent: for background location and push notifications, which you can revoke in your device settings at any time.
  • Legal obligation: where we must retain or disclose information by law.

5. Sharing

We do not sell personal information. We share information only:

  • Within the service: with the school, driver, and parents who are authorized to see a given student or bus.
  • Service providers: Google Firebase (authentication infrastructure, push messaging, analytics, crash reporting) and Google Maps (map display and place search). These providers process data under their own terms and privacy policies.
  • Legal and safety: when required by law, or to protect the safety of a child or other person.
  • Business transfer: if the service is transferred, information may be transferred as part of that transaction, subject to this Policy.

6. Children's privacy

Busivo is a tool for schools and parents. Student records are created and managed by the school or parent. We do not permit children to create accounts, and we do not knowingly collect information directly from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information directly to us, contact us and we will delete it.

Schools and parents are responsible for obtaining any consent required by their local law before entering a student's information.

7. Data retention

  • Account and school records are kept while the account is active and for a reasonable period afterward to meet legal and operational requirements.
  • Live location history is retained only as long as needed to provide the tracking feature and is then deleted or aggregated.
  • Crash and analytics data are retained according to Firebase's configured retention periods.
  • You may request deletion of your account and associated data by contacting us.

8. Security

We use HTTPS for all network traffic, signed Android releases, restricted server access, and industry-standard authentication. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we work to protect your information and review our safeguards regularly.

9. Your choices and rights

You can:

  • Disable location, notification, or background location permissions in Android settings. Note that disabling background location prevents the driver tracking feature from working.
  • Request access, correction, or deletion of your information.
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.

To exercise these rights, contact us at abdallah.aladl.dev@gmail.com. We may ask you to verify your identity. If your account is managed by a school, we may refer your request to the school.

10. International transfers

Our service providers may process information in countries other than your own. Where required, we use appropriate safeguards for those transfers.

11. Changes

We may update this Policy. We will post the new version at this URL and update the effective date. Material changes will be communicated through the app or by email where appropriate.
